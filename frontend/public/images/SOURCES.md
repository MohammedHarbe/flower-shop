# Image Sources and Licensing

No third-party or demo product photos were downloaded or added for the demo catalog in this pass. The seeded demo products keep `image_url` empty and use the storefront placeholder until the shop supplies approved images.

The existing `hero-flowers.jpg` and `toneflowers-logo.jpg` assets were already present in the project. Their original source and license are not recorded here, so confirm the shop's rights to use them commercially before production publication. Do not assume an image is licensed because it is publicly accessible.
